## Platform

web

## Users

Students, researchers, and learners capturing web content while studying or researching. They save, organize, and turn clips into useful knowledge.

## Product Purpose

Clipper Pro transforms web reading into actionable knowledge. It lets people capture important text from the web, organize it in a personal library, and generate AI-powered summaries on demand. Success means users can move from scattered reading to organized, reusable understanding with minimal friction.

## Positioning

Clipper Pro combines a low-friction web clipping workflow with privacy-first, explicit-on-demand AI: content is captured and organized without automatically sending it through AI, and summaries are generated only when the user asks for them.

## Operating Context

Users capture content while browsing the web, return to saved clips in a personal library, inspect clip details, and request summaries in formats such as bullets, paragraphs, or study notes. Authentication uses Google OAuth, and AI activity is governed by monthly usage limits.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the product name “Clipper Pro”.
- Use “clip”, “library”, and “summary” as core product terminology.
- Support web content capture, personal clip organization, clip detail views, and on-demand AI summaries.

The product name is Clipper Pro. The product presents itself as a modern learning companion with a privacy-first stance.
